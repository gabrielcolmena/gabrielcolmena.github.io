Pitch

Hirocato is an adventure-platformer game where you control a cat and have to parkour over platforms to deliver food. The game is loaded with hard-to-beat levels, great music, hand-made pixel art, and an emotional story.

About This Game

Master your parkour skills to swiftly deliver food (and other items). Jump, dash, climb, dodge, and move with agility to complete each delivery as fast as possible.

Story Mode

Deliver food and help others with special requests while discovering the unsettling truth of this animal-driven metropolis. Why is there a Pig running a ramen shop? Why didn't Cato evolve like the other animals? Where are the humans?!

Arcade Mode

Arcade mode is all about speed — jump and meow. Replay your favorite routes while trying to get your name on the online scoreboard.

Ghost Race Mode

Our favorite mode by far — download other players' best runs and try to beat them in a classic ghost race.

Scoreboards

Stamp your mark on the game's scoreboard. Each route has an online scoreboard to compete with other players.
