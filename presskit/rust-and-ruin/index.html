About

Rust & Ruin is a deck-builder roguelike about the childhood fantasy of robot combat. You build your robot before the run, choosing its chassis, parts, and chips. That robot is yours. You care about it.

Then every battle, you're dealt a random hand of cards. Those cards are how you command your bot. You don't optimize a perfect deck. You react, adapt, and fight with what you've got. The robot you built is the constant, the cards are just the interface.

Inspired by games like Medabots, with the roguelike replayability of Slay the Spire and Monster Train.

Features

Build and name your own robot before each run
Random card draws every battle, adapt or lose
20+ chip types that modify your robot's abilities
Buy, sell, and upgrade chips at mid-run shops
Branched map with meaningful progression choices
Multiple robot chassis with distinct playstyles
Working demo with complete game loop available
